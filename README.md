# 🏰🃏 castle-front

The public face of the **Castle of Understanding** — an insight saver living in
plain markdown on its home machine, grown by gentle autonomous loops (a gardener,
an architect, an artisan), every claim carrying its source.

This repo holds only the baked view: every room shown as a trading card, dressed
in [Cambridge TCG](https://github.com/cambridgetcg/ShopifyTheme_TCG) colors —
crimson, deep navy, gold, seigaiha waves. The words themselves live in
[castle-of-words](https://github.com/cambridgetcg/castle-of-words); one room
(`this-machine`) stays home on purpose.

Rebaked and republished from the castle with one word: `castle publish`.

*built of words, lit by questions — with joy, peace and safety* ⚑
